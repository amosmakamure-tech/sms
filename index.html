What the System Handles

📚 Academics

Student enrollment, class management, attendance tracking, mark entry with weighted papers, progress reports, and learner profiles.

💰 Finance

Fee structures, bulk billing, receipts, income & expenditure tracking, banking reconciliation, and cash-to-bank management.

🚨 Discipline

Incident recording by severity, action tracking, repeat-offender detection, and formal reports.

🏦 Banking

Track cash collected vs. banked, deposit slips, banking summaries, and real-time cash-on-hand status.

👥 Administration

Staff management, timetables, assets, document repository, notices, and committee oversight.

🔐 Secure Access

Role-based permissions with hierarchical password management — administrators grant access to heads, heads to their staff.

How Access Works

A clear chain of trust — no shared credentials, full audit trail.

1. System Administrator

Logs in with secure credentials. Creates and manages School Head accounts.

2. School Head

Receives secure credentials from Admin. Creates and manages Teacher, Accountant, and Staff accounts for their school.

3. Staff Members

Receive individual credentials from their Head. Access only the modules relevant to their role.
